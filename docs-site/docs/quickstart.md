# Prosa Quick Start

## 1. What Prosa is
Prosa is a writing workspace for long-form projects.

You can:
- Build projects with parts, chapters, and scenes.
- Draft in a focused editor with autosave.
- Keep scene notes, scene cards, and story metadata.
- Use AI writing support for rewriting, translation, and story coaching.
- Export your manuscript to common formats.

## 2. Creating an account
1. Go to [https://prosa-app.com](https://prosa-app.com).
2. Click **Start free** or **Sign in**.
3. Sign in with your account provider (Google/Microsoft, depending on your setup).
4. After sign-in, Prosa opens your workspace.

## 3. Creating your first project
1. Open **Projects**.
2. Click **New project**.
3. Enter a name.
4. Use the **Navigator** to add structure:
   - Add **Part**
   - Add **Chapter**
   - Add **Scene**
5. Click **Open manuscript** or **Resume** to start writing.

Tip: If onboarding is enabled, Prosa can create your first starter structure for you.

## 4. Writing your first scene
1. Open a scene from **Projects > Navigator**.
2. In the editor, start typing your draft.
3. Use the top toolbar for formatting (headings, lists, links, images, tables).
4. Use **Focus mode** if you want fewer distractions.
5. Your work saves automatically.

## 5. Using AI tools
1. Open the right panel in the editor.
2. Go to **Writing > Writing tools**.
3. Select text (for selection tools) or leave selection empty (for section tools).
4. Choose an AI action, for example:
   - Rewrite
   - Tighten
   - Expand
   - Change tone
   - Translate
   - Propose next paragraph
5. Review the suggestion and click **Apply** or **Discard**.

You can also use:
- **Scene card Coach** for scene metadata suggestions.
- **Consistency Coach** for character/place/timeline checks.
- **Style & quality Coach** for clarity and readability issues.

## 6. Exporting your work
1. In the editor, open **Document actions**.
2. Click **Export...**.
3. Choose:
   - Content (Document or Synopsis)
   - Format (HTML, Markdown, PDF, DOCX, EPUB when enabled)
   - Scope (whole manuscript, current section/page, selected sections, or selected text)
4. Optional: include title page, TOC, chapter break rules, and template settings.
5. Click **Preview** to check output.
6. Click **Export**.
