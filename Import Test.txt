Back at their base, Elena packed quickly, her mind racing. Priority-one meant something major—something serious enough to divert attention from the escalating conflict in the Arctic. As she placed her belongings into a small travel bag, Lars appeared in her doorway, worry evident on his usually stoic face.
"Be careful, Elena," he warned softly. "The politics behind all this... it's becoming dangerous."
She smiled slightly, grateful for his genuine concern. "I'll try."
He nodded solemnly, stepping aside as Sofia approached. Her earlier anger had now faded into worry. "Whatever this is," Sofia said quietly, "don’t let them manipulate you. You’re too important—too good—to lose to politics."
Elena paused, meeting Sofia’s intense gaze. "I promise," she assured gently. "I won’t let them."
With a reassuring squeeze of Sofia’s shoulder, Elena turned toward the waiting helicopter. The rotors spun furiously, throwing snow violently into the air. She took a deep breath, bracing herself against the gusts and stepped aboard.
The helicopter ascended swiftly into the sky, and Elena watched the base, shrinking rapidly into insignificance against the vast, frozen wilderness. She wondered briefly how long it would remain a refuge—how long before conflicts tore even these remote places apart.
